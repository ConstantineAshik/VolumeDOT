Keep your phone's volume controls within reach with a customizable floating button.

VolumeDot gives you a compact volume panel over other apps, with media, ring and alarm controls. Choose a horizontal or vertical slider, mute media, drag the controller by its handle, or let it snap to a screen edge.

Make it yours:
* Material 3 Expressive screens with light, dark and system themes
* Optional wallpaper-based colors on Android 12 and later
* Eight fallback accents, multiple bubble shapes and six icon modes
* Preset or custom emojis, adjustable size, corners and opacity
* Configurable auto-hide, idle dimming, touch feedback and motion

Use gestures to open controls, move the bubble, adjust volume while its position is locked, or mute media. Optional screenshot accessibility support lets you take a screenshot with a double tap while the controller is hidden.

Overlay access is needed to display the floating controller. Screenshot accessibility is optional and used only for the screenshot action. Notifications provide controller actions; optional unrestricted battery access may improve background availability on some phones. System and manufacturer background restrictions still apply.

Supports Android 9 and later. No account, advertisements or analytics are included.
